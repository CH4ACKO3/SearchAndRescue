This update adds automatic CASEVAC rescue and clearer field-treatment boundaries.

- Enable Field Rescue and CASEVAC work to automatically evacuate unattended casualties, then join existing teams or upgrade nearby SAR bed transports. Fix team membership, helper speed checks, bed reservation cleanup and medical-worker reservation races. Without an available bed, rescuers use single-pawn transport to a designated rescue point.
- Field tending now focuses on bleeding and supported emergencies, including dangerous infections and More Injuries early hypovolemic shock. Minor wounds wait until a safe bed or rescue point. Specialized emergency interventions remain available.
- Add a high-priority emergency medical care subtask under Doctor. With Work Tab, assign emergency-only medics while leaving routine tending and surgery to other doctors.
- Improve combined medical-mod behavior: per-wound medicine policies, shared blood/device budgets, external care ownership and CE supply protection. The compatibility panel remains organized by individual mod.

Validated with a 21-mod runtime setup, automated scheduling regressions, actual CASEVAC following and delivery, reservation lifecycle checks and destination-loss fallback tests. Optional integrations still require their respective mods; Work Tab is only needed for separate subtask assignments.
