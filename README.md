# Search and Rescue

[Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3796056278) · RimWorld 1.6 · Alpha

Search and Rescue adds three stackable battlefield orders to RimWorld 1.6:

- **Field treatment** stabilizes a humanlike pawn or non-hostile animal where they fell, one treatment round at a time.
- **Battlefield rescue** carries them to a suitable human or animal bed, or to a player-set rescue point when no bed is available.
- **Capture here** converts a downed hostile pawn into a prisoner without first requiring a prisoner bed.

Ordinary beds accepted by RimWorld's rescue search complete evacuation as well as medical beds; unresolved treatment remains eligible independently. A casualty already at the fallback rescue point is not carried to that same point again. Moving the point or making a bed available permits onward evacuation.

The Orders tab exposes one **Search and rescue** command. Left-click applies every applicable stage as the capture + treatment + rescue shortcut; right-click selects an individual stage, manages the fallback rescue point, or clears all Search and Rescue orders.

Capture remains a hard safety prerequisite, while treatment and transport compete in one worker-to-casualty maximum-weight graph. A doctor edge prefers field stabilization, but a marked casualty may begin evacuation when no doctor is available. If a doctor later wins that casualty, the carrier stops and drops them, the doctor treats them in place, and a rescue worker may wait nearby; waiting and moving another casualty are matched as competing transport tasks. Estimated doctor travel, remaining bandaging rounds, current treatment-toil time, and Medical Tend Speed determine when the carrier starts toward the patient. Once travel begins, the exact doctor/job pair is the damping commitment, and an interrupted treatment releases the standby on the next tick. After stabilization, continuity weight strongly prefers the same carrier for resumed evacuation. Treatment while physically moving is intentionally not attempted because a carried pawn is despawned and cannot participate safely in RimWorld's tending reservations.

A pawn with any Search and Rescue order is excluded from autonomous vanilla rescue, while explicit player jobs remain authoritative. A player-forced worker is never rematched until that order ends. Manually tending a marked casualty counts as the treatment claim and its actual health progress can complete field stabilization; manually carrying one preserves all applicable markers while the pawn is temporarily despawned, then cleanup resumes after drop-off.

Treatment is required only while a casualty exceeds the stabilization threshold; there is no mandatory first bandage for minor wounds. With a combined treatment-and-rescue order, the treatment designation remains as a dormant health watch until safe delivery. Crossing the threshold again during evacuation makes the casualty eligible for doctor interception, causing the carrier to stop and put them down before treatment. Routine doctors can finish minor wounds later. Anti-thrashing is part of the same score: keeping a pending pair, honouring a newly accepted route, getting close to the current patient, and avoiding another turn immediately after a switch all add smoothly decaying continuity weight. A new casualty can redirect a travelling doctor whenever its real matching benefit exceeds that continuity weight. Doctors already bandaging never switch mid-action.

Medical logistics use the same rolling-horizon assignment model. Each marked casualty receives a short-lived care plan containing the estimated essential medicine rounds, special device/consumable needs, and blood-loss deadline. For every doctor/patient edge, direct dry first aid competes with each medicine or device pickup: the intervention benefit is weighed against the complete detour, travel time, blood-loss slack, and resource scarcity. A winning pickup may amortize several nearby casualties' quota from that same stack (up to three patients and six consumable uses), but it may not append unscored pickup stops. Reusable devices and consumable stack quantities are leased in a shared resource ledger, so two workers cannot plan around the same defibrillator or the same medicine units. Claims expire automatically, and explicit player patient jobs or removed treatment orders release them immediately.

When the doctor detour would be wasteful, an unmarked internal supply task can compete with rescue and standby work in the Hauling graph, including while the doctor is already performing dry first aid. A hauler may pre-stage missing medicine or a special device beside the casualty; no extra designation or work column is exposed. A delivery never interrupts a partially filled tend bar: at the next successfully committed wound boundary, the delivered field stack competes with continued dry care, and a winning resource restarts treatment immediately with that stack. Supply follows the parent Hauling priority (including Work Tab's current-hour schedule), independently of the optional detailed battlefield-rescue row. The score accounts for survival benefit, complete pickup-to-patient route, resource scarcity, hauling priority, and the opportunity cost of diverting a skilled doctor. Supply, carrying, and waiting are mutually exclusive immediate roles for one casualty.

Scheduling work is tiered for performance. The tiny active-treatment and active-standby validity sets are checked per tick; designation and resource-lease maintenance runs every 60 ticks. Full care-plan construction and weighted matching use a 120-tick heartbeat, automatically relaxed to 180 ticks at 30 or more marked casualties. Designation changes, managed-job completion, rescue-point changes, and explicit player overrides mark the graph dirty and schedule an earlier rebuild after a 15-tick coalescing window. Idle work givers cannot bypass this cadence by repeatedly requesting unmatched work.

The mod adds a dedicated **Field Rescue** work column immediately before Doctor. All persistent SAR scheduling belongs to that ordinary, undrafted work graph; Doctor, Warden, Hauling, and optional Nursing priorities still gate the corresponding supporting lanes. The alpha never assigns or interrupts drafted pawns. A drafted search-and-rescue mode is deliberately outside this release.

## Compatibility

A categorized Chinese compatibility matrix, including official DLC behavior and known limits, is maintained in [`COMPATIBILITY.zh-CN.md`](COMPATIBILITY.zh-CN.md).

- Combat Extended: uses CE's native `Stabilize` only while at least one wound is actually stabilizable; follow-up/non-bleeding care falls back to ordinary tending. A full CE stabilization pass remains one atomic stage and consumes one medicine, shared-stack reservations match vanilla tending, CE bulk capacity is respected for mission kits, mech doctors are kept off CE's human-skill-only driver, and CE loadouts cannot reclaim or discard active SAR supplies. Medicine held by the doctor or casualty is used directly; third-pawn, pack-animal and vehicle cargo is routed through SAR restocking/delivery because CE's native driver cannot collect those holders as `targetB`.
- [RH2] First Aid: uses `CP_FirstAid` when installed and CE is not active.
- [RH2] Arrest Here!: uses `CP_ImprisonInPlace` when installed.
- [RH2] CASEVAC and other rescue mods: uses separate designations and vanilla hauling/reservation APIs, without patching their float menus or work givers.
- Nurse Job: adds `Nursing preferred` and `Nursing only` rescue workforce modes. Hauling remains the default. Marked fixed-effect supportive care (transfusion/infusion, hemostatic agents, bandages, and tourniquets) uses Nursing with Doctor fallback; skill-sensitive resuscitation and ordinary tending remain Doctor work.
- Smart Medicine - Continued: uses its medicine selection for each one-use stabilization round. When both Smart Medicine and RH2 First Aid are active, Smart Medicine's vanilla tending job takes precedence so inventory medicine remains valid.
- Pharmacist: Represcribed: applies its severity and population-specific advice (colonist, prisoner, slave, animal, entity, or guest) to doctor kits, implicit supply runs, CE stabilization, and the final tend. The individual pawn's vanilla medical-care limit remains a hard upper bound; `No meds` permits dry tending, while `No care` suppresses the field-treatment stage. Smart Medicine's own Pharmacist bridge remains authoritative when both are active.
- Medical Tab - Continued and 1trickPwnyta's Defaults: both edit RimWorld's native per-pawn or population-default medical-care fields, which Search and Rescue reads on every care-plan rebuild. Changes therefore apply without a separate cached policy or compatibility patch.
- Choose Your Medicine: reads its current pawn group, highest-priority hediff, severity/immunity stage, detailed medicine order, and one-off hediff override. That order governs the selected treatment resource, doctor kit, implicit supply preference, CE inventory use, `No meds`, and `No care`. Choose Your Medicine declares Smart Medicine and Pharmacist incompatible, so this provider is used in their alternative loadout rather than combined with them.
- Move the Patient: asks its patient component for the best priority-appropriate medical bed, then falls back to vanilla bed selection.
- Treat Dying First: Search and Rescue has its own marked-patient matcher and does not patch the mod's ordinary patient search.
- Priority Treatment Ressurected: Search and Rescue registers RH2/CE stabilization jobs as doctor work so the emergency scheduler does not interrupt an active treatment round. Its normal emergency selection remains intact.
- Yokai Village and other animal-race mods: non-hostile flesh animals can be treated and rescued, their animal beds and `MedicineBase` supplies use vanilla selection, and rescue-trained animals can respond through their normal rescue ThinkTree. Capture remains humanlike-only.
- More Injuries (Continued): builds explicit field-care demands for suction, defibrillation, epinephrine, tourniquets, hemostatic agents/bandages, saline, and blood. Its native one-shot device jobs and transfusion dispatchers are used, including their research gates and special job parameters; CPR remains the equipment-free fallback where medically applicable. Additional interventions such as splints remain available through More Injuries' own WorkGivers and are recognized as external treatment ownership instead of being blanket-blocked. Hemorrhagic stroke receives an ordinary temporary tend before evacuation, while lung collapse is treated as a surgery-only emergency and strongly boosts rescue priority instead of attempting an impossible field bandage.
- Emergency Transfusions: uses its native one-pack field transfusion job and supports packs carried by the casualty, doctor, map, or pack animal.
- Smarter Capture Them: its capture/transport WorkGiver participates in the common ownership gate, so autonomous jobs do not race a marked SAR stage; forced player orders still win.
- MedPod: MedPod rescue/carry jobs are recognized as transport ownership, assigned MedPods count as external treatment facilities, and both direct wardening scans and a patient's self-entry `NonScanJob` yield while SAR owns the matching stage.
- Dubs Rimkit: its `TendSelf` self-bandaging and `BandageOthers` jobs are recognized as treatment ownership so a manually started kit action safely overrides and releases an SAR claim.
- Trauma Team Complete: an active Trauma Team treatment Lord owns its contracted patient before the first reservation or job exists, so SAR yields without reservation races. Ownership ends when the team leaves its tend-and-hunt phase, allowing marked follow-up care to resume. Its private duty ThinkNode participates in the common ownership gate, and its 350-tick medic watchdog accepts every registered treatment/transport job targeting the client, including CE stabilization and More Injuries devices or transfusions. Trauma Team responders remain outside SAR worker matching and their carried supplies are not pooled into colony kits.
- Vehicle Framework: medicine and special first-aid devices in a stationary player vehicle's cargo participate in the same route, scarcity and soft-claim calculations as map supplies. Doctors may restock a mission kit from the vehicle, and haulers may extract a claimed quantity for direct delivery. Extraction uses Vehicle Framework's public cargo API so its cargo mass/stat caches and events stay valid; moving, despawned, hostile or unreachable vehicles are excluded. Vehicle cargo remains available with Combat Extended enabled.
- Delivered and pre-existing nearby field supplies retain persistent per-casualty quantity references. Each care-plan rebuild reconciles those references to the exact current medicine/device budget, so a full nearby stack satisfies only the required quota and merged stacks divide their remaining units without double-counting. Ordinary hauling and mission-kit/supply searches leave a referenced stack in place; explicit player hauling and single-round medical use by an associated casualty remain allowed. References are released individually when the budget falls, treatment completes, the treatment mark is removed, the casualty dies or leaves the field, or the stack is consumed/moved.
- Mission-kit restocking can obtain medicine and special first-aid devices from another same-faction pawn's inventory. The carrier is reserved for the handoff, resources claimed by another responder or targeted by the carrier's current job are excluded, and the exact claimed count transfers directly into the receiving doctor's inventory.
- EPOE-Forked and Medical System Expansion 2: their prostheses, sub-parts, medicine stats, and operation bills remain native. Search and Rescue does not attempt implant/replacement surgery in the field; it evacuates life-threatening, non-tendable conditions to a bed for the normal surgery system.
- Death Rattle Continued: any current life-threatening stage contributes urgency even when blood-loss time is unavailable, so organ failure and clinical-death states move ahead of routine casualties. EPOE organ replacement remains a normal bed surgery.

## Development

This repository contains the mod's source, definitions, translations, and artwork. Subscribe on Steam Workshop for the playable package; compiled binaries and third-party assemblies are not committed here.

Build with the .NET SDK and .NET Framework 4.8 targeting support, using assemblies from your own RimWorld 1.6 and Harmony installations. Open `Source/SearchAndRescue.sln`, or override the local dependency paths from the command line:

```powershell
dotnet build Source/SearchAndRescue/SearchAndRescue.csproj -c Release `
  -p:RimWorldManagedDir="C:\Games\RimWorld\RimWorldWin64_Data\Managed" `
  -p:HarmonyAssemblyPath="C:\Games\Harmony\Current\Assemblies\0Harmony.dll"
```

Replace the example paths with your installed locations. The compiled assembly is copied to `Assemblies/`. Optional integrations use runtime detection; their assemblies are not required to compile SAR.

Run `dotnet run --project Tools/SchedulerSimulation/SchedulerSimulation.csproj -c Release` with the .NET 8 SDK for deterministic skill/severity, scarce-device fallback, supply-vs-rescue, and randomized matching regressions. This simulation project does not require the game assemblies. See [in-game testing](TESTING.zh-CN.md) for the live regression fixtures.
